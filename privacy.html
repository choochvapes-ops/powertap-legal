Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

PowerTap ("we", "us", or "our") operates the PowerTap mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights regarding your data.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact Information: Your name and email address when you create an account.
  • Account Identifiers: A unique user ID assigned to your account to identify you within our system.
  • Precise Location: Your device's GPS location while the App is actively in use, to show nearby PowerTap stations on the map. We do not collect location data when the App is closed or running in the background.
  • Payment Information: Payment details (such as card information) are entered directly into Stripe's secure interface. We receive only a payment confirmation and transaction reference — we do not see, store, or transmit your full card number, CVV, or banking credentials.
  • Rental History: Records of power bank rentals, return times, and charges associated with your account.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and push notification token used to send rental alerts and return reminders.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process power bank rentals and payments
  • To show nearby PowerTap stations on the map based on your location
  • To send push notifications for rental reminders and return alerts
  • To provide customer support
  • To detect and prevent fraud or misuse of the service
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To improve the App and our services

3. Tracking

We do not track you. PowerTap does not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites. We do not use your data for targeted advertising, and we do not share your data with data brokers or advertising networks.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with trusted third-party service providers as necessary to operate the App:

  • Stripe: For secure payment processing. Stripe receives payment details directly and is subject to its own Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps Platform: To display maps and nearby station locations. Google is subject to its own Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Sign in with Google): If you choose to sign in with Google, Google authenticates your identity. We receive only your name and email address from Google.
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple): If you choose to Sign in with Apple, Apple authenticates your identity. We receive only the information Apple provides (name and email). We use this solely for account creation and authentication — never for advertising.
  • Firebase (Google): For sending push notifications to your device.

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and may not use it for their own purposes beyond the services they provide to us.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Rental transaction records are retained for up to 7 years as required for financial record-keeping. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

6. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time in two ways:

  • In-App: Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account within the PowerTap app.
  • By email: Contact us at support@powertap.ca with the subject line "Delete My Account".

Upon deletion, your personal data will be removed from our active systems within 30 days. Some anonymized transaction data may be retained for legal and financial compliance purposes.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • TLS encryption for all data transmitted between the App and our servers
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plain text
  • Payment data is handled entirely by Stripe and never stored on our servers
  • Access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel only

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Opt-out: Opt out of marketing or promotional communications at any time
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@powertap.ca.

9. Children's Privacy

PowerTap is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with their information, please contact us at support@powertap.ca.

10. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of Canada, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation. By using the App, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of your information as described in this policy.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy in the App and updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
